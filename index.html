HOW TO CHESS
PAWN
The pawn is the most basic piece in chess. It can only move forward one square at a time, except on its first move when it can move two squares. Pawns capture diagonally and can promote to any other piece when they reach the opposite end of the board.
KNIGHT
The knight moves in an L-shape, going two squares in one direction and then one square perpendicular. It is the only piece that can jump over other pieces.
